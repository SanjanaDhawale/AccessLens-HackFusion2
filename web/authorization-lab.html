Role-Based Authorization Lab

Local synthetic ConnectedCar test fixture · BOLA and BFLA · no external traffic

1. Synthetic test identities

ConnectedCar uses an X-Demo-User header to simulate identities. These are not passwords or real accounts.

IdentityDemo roleExpected permission
aliceownerRead CAR-001 and her profile
bobownerRead CAR-002 and his profile
adminadminRead admin settings and protected resources

For this first version, identities are fixed to known synthetic fixtures; user-provided credentials are not accepted or stored.
