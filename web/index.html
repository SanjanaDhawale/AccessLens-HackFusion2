HOW TO READ THIS MATRIX

Policy is not proof of runtime behaviour.

Allowed means the configured policy expects access. Own only means access is conditional on resource ownership or an authorized maintenance relationship. Denied means the identity should not access that operation. Actual results must be measured by the test engine; untested combinations stay inconclusive.
